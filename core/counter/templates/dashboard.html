MECHANICAL ALIGNMENT

Find every number.

Line up each digit in its window and save that servo position.

Digit positions

Pulse widths are specific to your servo and mechanism. Suggested values are unsaved starting points. Test gently; stop before a mechanical end stop.

A full-turn digit wheel needs a position-controlled servo with sufficient travel. Continuous-rotation servos cannot select a digit without position feedback.

Saving stops outputs. Arm again for the next test. Blank positions stay uncalibrated.

Servo power is separate from logic power.

Power the Pi through USB-C. Power servo V+ from a regulated supply sized for your servos, with a shared ground. Do not power sixteen servos from the Pi’s GPIO header.

Use a pull-up from OE to 3.3 V to keep outputs disabled while the Pi boots. Stop outputs disables the signal; it does not cut servo power.
